Data and third parties

A plain list of every service that can see something from your device.

What we collect

Nothing. X Sword has no server of its own that receives your data, no advertising identifiers and no usage tracking.

Services that can see something

Website: the X Sword website (these documents and the home page) is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub receives the usual web request information (such as your IP address and browser type) under GitHub's privacy statement. The app itself loads nothing from the website: the game, its fonts and its sounds are inside the app.

Matchmaking (online play only): the PeerJS cloud service sees your IP address and the room code while a connection is set up.

Connection helpers (online play only): STUN servers (run by Google and PeerJS) learn your IP address so two devices can find each other; relay (TURN) servers run by PeerJS may carry encrypted game traffic when a direct connection is not possible.

Platform services (phone apps only, if you sign in): Apple Game Center and iCloud, or Google Play Games.

Stores (phone apps): Apple and Google receive your download and, in future, purchases, under their own policies.

What other players see

Your display name and color, your moves, and (through the direct connection) your IP address. Choose a name that does not reveal personal information.

Nothing is sold or shared for advertising

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
